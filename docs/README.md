# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Project Management Processes

OctoAcme manages cross-functional projects through an iterative lifecycle of initiation, planning, execution, release, and continuous improvement. Initiation validates the business need, defines measurable outcomes, identifies stakeholders and resources, and establishes whether work should proceed. Planning turns approved work into a prioritized, estimated backlog with acceptance criteria and a Definition of Done, while mapping milestones, dependencies, and risks.

During execution, teams track work on a project board and coordinate through standups, delivery reviews, and regular PM–Product Manager syncs. The Project Manager coordinates schedules, risks, dependencies, and communications; the Product Manager owns outcomes, prioritization, and success measures; Developers build and test changes; QA/Testing validates acceptance criteria; and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Teams use small pull requests linked to issues, conduct code review, and escalate blockers through the team, PM, Product Lead, and sponsor as appropriate.

Quality assurance runs throughout delivery: teams use unit and integration tests, end-to-end smoke tests for critical flows, security scanning, manual QA when needed, and CI checks. Before release, they confirm acceptance criteria, passing CI and security scans, release notes, a rollback or mitigation plan, and prepared smoke tests. They deploy to staging for verification, deploy to production, complete post-deployment checks, and communicate the release to stakeholders and support.

After each sprint, release, milestone, or incident, teams hold a retrospective to capture what went well, what could improve, and a small set of actionable improvements with owners, due dates, and success criteria. Progress, risks, blockers, and decisions are kept visible through status updates, the risk register, project artifacts, and dashboards; improvement actions are tracked in the backlog and revisited in regular syncs.
